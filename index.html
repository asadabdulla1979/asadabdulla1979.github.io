The third of a harvest we never take

Pakistan grew 3.7% in FY26 and called it a recovery. Agriculture grew 2.9%, the widest miss of any sector, and the reason is not the weather. It is the gap between what our farms produce and what the same seed and soil already yield on the country's best fields, a prize worth close to a tenth of GDP, and the first reform in years to name it.
