Assumptions in this tab

Ex-factory list prices, July 2026 (hybrid/PHEV 25% prices are PakWheels' computed pass-through of 7 July; the 9% scenario is approximated from the ex-tax value). EV efficiency 5.0-6.8 km/kWh per model including ~10% charging losses; PHEV electric efficiency and hybrid-mode fuel economy per model; HEV fuel economy per model. Maintenance (separate toggle) is tiered by vehicle size and class: Rs 2.4-4.5/km petrol (a Swift costs less to service than a Fortuner), 1.2-1.8 EV, 2.2-2.8 PHEV/REEV, 2.4-2.7 HEV; it favours the electric option. Insurance (separate toggle) 1.75%/yr of value (petrol) vs 2.2% (electric); it usually favours the petrol option, since the electric car is dearer. Battery provision Rs 2.0/km (EV/PHEV/REEV) or Rs 1.0/km (HEV) against post-warranty replacement; packs carry ~8-year/160,000 km warranties. Registration and token taxes broadly at parity, excluded. USD at ~278 PKR. Verify prices before purchase.

Assumptions in this tab

10 kW turnkey systems were quoted around Rs 850,000-945,000 installed in mid-2026, hence the Rs 90,000/kW default; override to match your quote. Generation default 4.2 units/kW/day. Solar units sent to the car are valued at the ~Rs 11 net-billing export price you forgo; charging not covered by solar is billed at Rs 57 (top slab). Petrol at Rs 310.71. PHEVs charge only their electric share (70%) this way; their remaining kilometres burn petrol at the model's hybrid-mode economy. Joint payback divides system cost plus vehicle premium by the combined annual saving versus the petrol benchmark. USD at ~278 PKR.
